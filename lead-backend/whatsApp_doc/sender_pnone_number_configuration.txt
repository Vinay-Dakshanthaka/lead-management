### **Conclusion**

1. **Multiple Sender Phone Numbers**:
   - You can configure multiple **sender phone numbers** in your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) through the Meta Developer Console. 
   - Each phone number will have a unique `phone_number_id`.

2. **Dynamic Use of Phone Numbers**:
   - You can dynamically specify the `phone_number_id` in the WhatsApp Cloud API endpoint:
     ```
     POST https://graph.facebook.com/v17.0/<phone_number_id>/messages
     ```
   - By dynamically changing `<phone_number_id>` in your web application, you can send messages from different configured numbers.

3. **Integration in Application**:
   - Use your application's logic (e.g., Node.js backend) to:
     - Fetch the appropriate `phone_number_id` based on your lead’s interest or requirements.
     - Send the API request using the selected `phone_number_id` for messaging.

4. **Flexibility**:
   - This approach allows you to target leads with specific sender numbers as per your business logic, such as:
     - Region-based numbers.
     - Dedicated numbers for certain campaigns or agents.
